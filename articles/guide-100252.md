# Content Moderation API: The Complete Guide (2026)

> Learn what content moderation APIs detect, how they work, how to choose a provider, and what to consider when comparing pricing and use cases.



## What a content moderation API is

A content moderation API is a service you send user-generated content to and get back a real-time decision: whether it violates your policy, which category it hits, and how confident the system is. It lets you add moderation with a single HTTP call instead of building and hosting your own models.

## How it works

You POST a message or image to the API. It normalizes the text to address evasion such as leetspeak and Unicode look-alikes, runs it through classifiers or a language model, and scores it across categories. If a score crosses your threshold, you get a flag and a reason, and your app decides what to do: block, hide, warn, or queue for review. Some systems let you define the policy yourself rather than use a fixed taxonomy.

## What it detects

Common categories include hate and discrimination, harassment, sexual content, violence, self-harm, dangerous content, personally identifiable information (PII), profanity, and spam. Platforms with younger users may also need grooming detection. A moderation API may let you tune each category's threshold and add a custom rule for anything the standard categories miss.

## How to choose one

- **Content type:** text, image, voice, or video, since not every API covers all of them.
- **Fixed categories vs. custom policy:** can you express your exact rules, or only choose from the provider’s categories?
- **Compliance:** PII redaction, COPPA, and EU or UK data residency for GDPR.
- **Cost model:** per-1,000 pricing, free tier, minimums, and lock-in.
- **Latency and evasion resistance** for real-time chat.

## Pricing

Pricing varies by provider and content type. When comparing plans, check per-1,000 pricing, free tiers, minimums, and whether multimodal features are included. Verify current prices directly with each provider.

## By use case

- Gaming chat moderation: real-time detection with evasion resistance and age-appropriate safeguards.
- Forums and communities: moderate posts and comments at scale.
- Marketing campaigns: automatically moderate social and ad comments.
- Profanity filtering: go beyond static word lists.
- Unity and Roblox game integrations.

## How to get started

Test representative messages and images, then review the API documentation and integration requirements. Define your moderation policy, choose thresholds for each category, and decide how your app will handle flags, including whether to block, hide, warn, or queue content for review.
