# n8n vs Zapier: Which Is Actually Better for Business Automation?

> A comparison of n8n and Zapier covering cost, flexibility, AI capabilities, hosting options, and use cases for business automation.



n8n and Zapier both connect apps and automate workflows, but they serve different needs. This comparison covers cost, flexibility, AI capabilities, hosting options, and limitations.

## What each tool is

Zapier is a no-code automation platform for connecting apps without writing code. It offers more than 7,000 app integrations, a visual interface, and pricing based on task volume.

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool with a visual editor. It can be self-hosted or used through n8n Cloud. It supports code execution inside workflows, branching logic, AI agent nodes, and custom integrations through HTTP requests or JavaScript.

## Cost

Zapier pricing scales with task count. At 2,000 tasks per month on the Starter plan, the source lists a price of $29.99 per month. At 100,000 tasks per month on the Teams plan, it lists $103.50 per month. Costs can rise for high-volume workflows such as CRM syncing, bulk email processing, and lead routing.

n8n Cloud starts at $24 per month for 2,500 workflow executions, according to the source. Self-hosting is another option: the source estimates that running n8n on a $6-per-month VPS can make hosting costs relatively fixed. Actual costs depend on the server and workload.

## Flexibility and what you can build

Zapier is quick to set up for simple workflows, such as a trigger followed by an action. Two-step automations between popular apps can take minutes. More complex workflows may be limited by branching and error-handling options, and custom logic can require workarounds.

n8n supports multi-branch workflows, conditional logic, looping through arrays, JavaScript in workflow nodes, API requests with control over headers and authentication, reusable sub-workflows, and AI agent nodes. These features can support more complex automation, though they may require more technical setup.

## AI capabilities

n8n includes AI agent nodes for incorporating LLM calls into workflows. The source describes chaining AI steps, adding tool-calling capabilities, and creating workflows with human approval steps. For example, a workflow could classify an email, query a CRM, draft a reply, and wait for approval before sending it.

Zapier also offers AI features. The source characterizes them as suitable for tasks such as reformatting text or generating summaries, and says they offer less flexibility for building multi-step agentic workflows with custom LLM calls and memory.

## Where Zapier may be a better fit

Zapier is accessible and quick to set up. For small businesses automating simple tasks, such as sending email notifications to Slack or adding form submissions to a CRM, it may not require developer support. Its integrations and documentation can also help non-technical users get started.

For simple, low-volume automations between popular apps, Zapier may be a suitable choice. More complex requirements can make the choice less straightforward.

## Which one is right for your business

- Use Zapier if you need simple app-to-app automations, prefer a no-code setup, want to get started quickly, and have low task volumes.
- Use n8n if you need complex multi-step logic, want to build AI-powered workflows, run high task volumes, prefer self-hosting and data control, or are building automations for a client or enterprise context.
- Consider an n8n agency if you need n8n workflows but do not have in-house developers to build and maintain them.

## Choosing for a growing business

The source argues that many businesses outgrow Zapier and later migrate to n8n. Such a migration can require rebuilding workflows. If you expect your automation needs to grow, consider the potential migration effort when choosing a tool.

On the other hand, if you are not technical and will not have someone manage n8n, Zapier's ease of use may justify its higher cost. The right choice depends on which tool your team can maintain and use effectively.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is n8n really free?

n8n is open-source and can be self-hosted without a software subscription. You still pay for the server; the source estimates a basic VPS at $5–10 per month. n8n Cloud, the hosted version, starts at $24 per month, according to the source. Total costs depend on your setup and usage.

### Is n8n harder to use than Zapier?

n8n has a steeper learning curve. Its visual editor supports complex workflows, which can take time to learn. Many non-technical users may find Zapier easier. For business-critical automation, consider whether you have the expertise to build and maintain n8n workflows.

### Can n8n replace Zapier completely?

n8n may cover many business automation use cases, but the fit depends on the apps and integrations you need. Zapier has a larger selection of pre-built integrations, while n8n's HTTP request node and credential system can connect to APIs without a native integration. Check the required services and setup before deciding.

### What's the best n8n alternative?

Make (formerly Integromat) is another visual automation tool. The source describes it as more flexible than Zapier and more accessible than n8n, and suggests considering it for visual automations with moderate complexity. Compare current features and pricing against your requirements.

## Building n8n automations

Businesses planning to use n8n may need support building and maintaining workflows. Relevant use cases include CRM automation, AI workflows, lead routing, invoice processing, and social media scheduling.
